Form not working? Use the Bluesky app manually
  1. Open the Bluesky app (or bsky.app), start account creation.
  2. You'll see a line: "You are creating an account on Bluesky Social" — this looks like a label, but it's actually tappable. Tap it.
  3. Choose "Custom" and enter memo.dog as the hosting provider.
  4. Continue with handle, email, password, and the invite code as normal.

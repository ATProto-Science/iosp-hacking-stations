# Noizetoyz: from single notes to ensembles (draft)

So far we stream single notes via the atmosphere, and hence store strings of single notes on
the user's PDS. But does this make sense? Are ATProto personal data servers meant to store
pressed keys on a keyboard?

Let's go one step further and think in terms of presets, instruments and sequences, based on
the capabilities that Mozzi offers. Suggest some example instruments, e.g. an 808-inspired drum
machine, a 909 bass sequencer, or the Groovesizer presets (source available). Users want to
exchange instruments, sound presets and musical performance data.

Let's make a plan.

See also `~/src/ensembles` as a way to morph between data and make those things malleable.
This is the prerequisite step for Noizetoyz.
